## THE PROBLEM

### Your team is already using AI. Are they using it well?

Many employees use AI tools every day without any formal guidance. The result is uneven quality, wasted time, and avoidable risks with company data.

Khrien Academy closes that gap. We give your staff a clear, practical understanding of AI so they can save time, produce better work, and make smarter decisions, with confidence and with the right safeguards in place.

---

## WHAT YOUR TEAM WILL LEARN

Our programme is built on the AI Foundations curriculum, the same curriculum that has certified over 130 students through Khrien Academy. We adapt it to the way your organisation works.

**1. AI Foundations**
Understand what AI is, how modern AI tools work, and where they genuinely help in a working day.

**2. Prompt Engineering**
Learn how to write clear, effective prompts that produce reliable, high quality results, every time.

**3. AI for Everyday Work**
Use AI for writing, research, analysis, summarising, planning, and communication, with hands-on practice on real tasks.

**4. Building AI Workflows**
Turn one-off tricks into repeatable workflows your team can use daily to save hours each week.

**5. Responsible AI Use**
Protect company and client data, check AI output for accuracy, and know when not to use AI.
