There's no shortage of creative talent in Africa. What's missing is structured access — to real training, real mentorship, and a real path into the animation industry. That's exactly why Khrien Academy partnered with Animate Africa: to put studio-level animation training in front of people who'd never normally get the chance, at zero cost.
